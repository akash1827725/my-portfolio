About Me

My journey into technology and what drives me forward.

Hi! I'm Akash, a first-year B.E. Computer Science (Cyber Security) student at Dayananda Sagar Academy of Technology and Management (DSATM), Bengaluru. My journey into technology began with a genuine curiosity about how systems work — and more importantly, how they can be protected.

Currently, I'm building my programming foundations with C and Python, and diving into web development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Every line of code I write brings me closer to my goal of becoming a skilled cybersecurity professional.

I'm fascinated by the world of ethical hacking and artificial intelligence — fields that are shaping the future of technology. I believe the best way to learn is by building real things, breaking them, and understanding why they work. That mindset keeps me motivated to push through challenges and keep improving.

Outside of tech, I'm a multilingual individual who speaks Kannada, Tamil, Hindi, and English. I value hard work, curiosity, and continuous growth — qualities I carry into everything I do, both in academics and personal projects.
